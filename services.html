Services

Drywall Services Across Alaska

Every stage of the wall, handled by one experienced crew — hang, tape, texture, and finish. Here's exactly what we do and how we do it.

Sheetrock Hanging

Board goes up fast, square, and tight. We plan the layout to minimize butt joints, use the right length sheets for the room, and fasten to manufacturer spec so your finish doesn't pop screws a year later.

  • New construction, additions, garages, and shops
  • Standard, 5/8" fire-rated, and sound-dampening board
  • Ceilings, vaults, and curved surfaces

Taping & Mudding

The finish is where drywall jobs are won or lost. Our tapers run clean seams, feather wide coats, and sand under work lights so joints stay invisible after paint — not just before it.

  • Level 4 standard finish on all jobs
  • Corner bead — square, bullnose, and off-angle
  • Proper drying time between coats, even in winter

Level 5 Finishes

A full skim coat over the entire surface for glass-smooth walls. The right call for high-gloss or dark paint, big Alaska windows with raking light, and high-end interiors where nothing less will do.

  • Full-wall skim coat over a level 4 base
  • Recommended for sheen paints and accent walls
  • Inspection under angled light before we call it done

Texture & Texture Matching

From clean orange peel to hand-troweled knockdown, we spray and finish textures that are even across the whole surface — and we match existing textures on repairs so patches vanish.

  • Orange peel, knockdown, splatter, and smooth
  • Sample-panel test matches before we spray your wall
  • Ceiling texture, including popcorn removal and retexture

Drywall Repair

Doorknob holes, water stains, settling cracks, botched DIY patches — we cut out the damage, rebuild the surface, and blend texture and finish so you can't find the repair.

  • Water and ice-dam damage restoration
  • Crack repair that addresses the cause, not just the symptom
  • Small-job friendly — no repair is too minor to do right

Moisture-Resistant & Specialty Board

Alaska homes fight humidity swings all year. We install moisture-resistant board in baths, laundries, and kitchens, and spec fire-rated or sound-dampening board where it belongs.

  • Moisture-resistant (green) board for damp rooms
  • 5/8" Type X fire-rated board for garages and code walls
  • Honest guidance on where each board type is required

New Construction & Builder Packages

Builders get one call, one crew, and a schedule they can plan around. We stock, hang, tape, texture, and leave the site broom-clean — house after house.

  • Full-house hang-and-finish packages
  • Coordinated scheduling with your other trades
  • Dust containment and daily cleanup
