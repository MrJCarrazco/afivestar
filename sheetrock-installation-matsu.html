Hang, tape, and finish for new homes, additions, and shops across Wasilla, Palmer, Big Lake, and the whole valley. Family-run crew, straight quotes. Se habla español.

From Bare Studs to Paint-Ready Walls

New construction drywall is a schedule game: the framer is done, the electrician and plumber have passed inspection, and now the walls need to close up fast — without shortcuts that show up later as cracked seams or wavy joints under the living-room lights.

What our new-construction scope typically covers:

  • Board delivery coordination and stocking each room
  • Hanging — ceilings first, walls tight, screws set right
  • Taping and mudding to Level 4 (standard) or Level 5 (smooth wall)
  • Corner bead that stays straight through Alaska freeze–thaw cycles
  • Texture: orange peel, knockdown, or smooth
  • Garage and shop board, including fire-rated assemblies where code requires

Built for Alaska Schedules

We work new builds across the Mat-Su Valley year-round. Winter drywall is normal here — it just has to be done right: the structure needs stable heat and the mud needs real drying time between coats. We plan the visits around that instead of pretending Alaska is Arizona.

For builders and owner-builders

  1. Send the plans or square footage. Board count and a bid usually within a couple of days.
  2. Lock the window. We coordinate with your inspection schedule so the crew shows up when the house is actually ready.
  3. Hang, tape, texture. One crew start to finish — no handoffs, no mystery subs.

Doing an owner-build or a garage yourself and just want the taping done? We take finish-only jobs too — ask.
