## (a) Facebook — Marketplace & Mat-Su groups

**Key rule (verified in Meta's commerce policies):** *services may NOT be listed as regular Facebook Marketplace items* — "Offering services (e.g. house cleaning) is only allowed on Jobs on Marketplace." Contractors who post "drywall repair $X" as a Marketplace listing get removed. The real channel is **groups**, plus a business Page for credibility.

**What actually works for Mat-Su contractors (observed pattern):**
- Homeowners post "looking for a drywall/sheetrock guy" in local community groups constantly; the winner is whoever replies fast with a photo of real work and a phone number.
- Contractors posting their own ads works when it's photo-led ("before/after of a ceiling we patched in Wasilla this week — booking next month") and infrequent. Wall-of-text ads get scrolled past; daily posting gets you muted or banned (most BST groups have "no reposting within X days" rules — read each group's pinned rules first).

**Groups to join (search these names in Facebook — membership requires Juan's personal account):**
- Mat-Su Valley Buy Sell Trade (largest local BST; multiple variants exist — join the biggest 2)
- Mat-Su Valley Free Crap & Sell Things (active, verified exists)
- Mat-Su News and Info | Alaska (public, ~2.7K)
- Wasilla/Palmer community pages + "Alaska Contractors" / trades groups
- Search: "Wasilla classifieds", "Palmer AK buy sell", "Big Lake Alaska community"

**Exact steps for Juan:**
1. From his personal Facebook, create a **Business Page**: "A Five Star Drywall — Wasilla, AK". Add logo (in repo `assets/`), phone, site link, service area. ~15 min.
2. Join the 4–6 groups above (some ask screening questions — answer as a local resident/business). ~10 min, approval may take days.

4. Turn on notifications for the groups; **reply to "looking for drywall" posts within the hour** — that's where the real leads are.

**Cadence:** 1 self-post per group per month, max. Replying to requests: unlimited and always welcome.
**Time cost:** ~45 min setup, then ~15 min/day scanning.
**Expected flow:** in a market where "everyone else is invisible," realistically 2–6 warm inquiries/month once established. This is the highest-intent free channel in the valley.

## (b) Nextdoor — Business Page setup

Free. Also doubles as a citation (already listed in CITATIONS_AND_NAP.md).

**Steps (verified from business.nextdoor.com):**
1. Go to **nextdoor.com/create-business** (or from an existing neighbor account: profile photo → "Add Business Page").
2. Enter business name ("A Five Star Drywall"), category (Drywall/Home Improvement — up to 10 categories), address (can be **hidden** for home-based — leave "show address" unchecked), phone, email, website.
3. Add logo + cover photo (finished-wall photo). Pages with a logo get ~7× more views; with a photo gallery ~2.6×.
4. Add license # in the profile (trust signal Nextdoor explicitly recommends).
5. Write the "story": family business, Wasilla, bilingual EN/ES.
6. After **verification**, free **Business Posts** reach all neighbors within 2 miles, 2×/month.
7. Ask past customers who use Nextdoor for a **recommendation** — recommendations drive the whole platform (up to 19× page views).

**Recommended Nextdoor tip:** join as a neighbor too (Juan's personal account) — "looking for a contractor" recommendation threads are answerable and are the main lead source, same dynamic as Facebook.

**Time cost:** ~20 min setup. **Expected flow:** low in Mat-Su (Nextdoor density is thinner than Facebook here) — maybe 0–2 leads/month, but $0 and compounding via recommendations. Rank 4: do it, don't obsess over it.

## (c) Alaska's List (alaskaslist.com) — the Craigslist of Alaska

Verified from their own documentation (alaskaslist.com/readme/documentation.html):
- **No account/signup needed.** Post via the blue "Post Your Ad" button; they ask only email + phone.
- After posting you get a **private Ad Management Link** — SAVE IT (it's the only way to edit/delete the ad).
- **Free Ads:** human-moderated before publication; **one free ad per item/service — no repetitive re-posting/bumping** ("repetitive top posting of Free Ads is simply not permitted"). The ad stays up until you delete it, so one good ad can run indefinitely.
- **Featured Ads:** low-cost paid tier, shown above free ads, payments non-refundable. Skip until the free ad proves the channel.
- Photos: upload landscape-orientation JPGs (their staff manually re-orients bad ones).

## (e) Thumbtack / Angi economics for drywall — honest numbers

Researched September 2026 from multiple contractor-facing sources:

**Thumbtack**
- Pay-per-lead; algorithmic pricing. Range across home services: **$8–$150+/lead; most home-improvement trades land ~$25–75/lead.** You pay when a homeowner responds to/contacts you, no subscription and no contract.
- Leads are **shared** — the same homeowner pings several pros; typical close rates contractors report are ~1-in-5 to 1-in-10 for competitive trades.
- Realistic math for drywall repair: at ~$40/lead and 1-in-5 close, customer-acquisition cost ≈ $200. Fine for a $1,500 ceiling job; painful for a $250 patch. So **set a minimum job size** if used.
- Alaska wrinkle (honest unknown): thin pro competition in the Mat-Su could mean cheaper leads and better close rates than lower-48 averages — or simply very few leads because homeowner traffic is thin too. Nobody publishes AK-specific numbers.
- **Verdict: conditional.** No contract = low risk to test with a capped weekly budget (e.g. $50/wk) *after* free channels are running and *only* if Juan wants more volume. Requires Juan's account + payment card, so it's his call entirely.

**Angi / HomeAdvisor (Angi Leads)**
- Annual membership (~$300/yr commonly reported) **plus** $15–85+ per shared lead, and **12-month contracts**.
- Widely reported problems: billed for unreachable/duplicate leads, aggressive auto-spend, hard cancellation. Angi paid a **$7.2M FTC settlement (2023)** over misleading lead claims. Platform is shrinking: Angi's own filings show network leads down ~81% YoY after the Jan-2025 "homeowner choice" change.
- Counterpoint from our own market research: **A & A Wall Solutions in Wasilla built a real business on 30 HomeAdvisor reviews** — the *review profile* has value even though paid leads are a bad deal.
- **Verdict: NOT recommended for paid leads.** Claim the free listing only (already queued in CITATIONS_AND_NAP.md) and let organic reviews accumulate there. Do not sign the 12-month lead contract.
