# Google Business Profile — Setup Runbook for Juan
*Do this yourself on your phone or laptop — it needs YOUR Google sign-in (afivedrywall@gmail.com). ~15 minutes plus verification wait. It's free. This is the #1 thing that will bring calls.*

## Why
When someone in Wasilla googles "drywall repair near me", the top of the page is a **map with 3 businesses**. Those 3 get most of the clicks. Getting on that map only requires a free, verified Google Business Profile — and almost no drywall company in the valley has a good one.

## Step-by-step

1. Go to **https://business.google.com** → sign in with **afivedrywall@gmail.com**.
2. Click **Add your business** → business name: exactly **`A Five Drywall`**
   (Keep it consistent everywhere — see CITATIONS_AND_NAP.md. Don't stuff keywords like "A Five Drywall Wasilla Best Sheetrock" — Google suspends profiles for that.)
3. **Business category:**
   - Primary: **Drywall contractor**
   - Additional (add after setup in Edit profile → Business category): **Dry wall supply store? NO — skip.** Add: **Contractor**, **Construction company** (only if offered; keep it honest).
4. **"Do you want to add a location customers can visit?" → NO.**
   You work at customers' homes; choose **service-area business**. This hides the home address from the public listing (good for privacy) and is the correct setup.
5. **Service areas** (add all): Wasilla, Palmer, Big Lake, Houston, Meadow Lakes, Knik-Fairview, Butte, Sutton-Alpine, Anchorage, Eagle River, Chugiak. (Google allows up to 20; these cover the real driving range.)
6. **Contact info:** phone **(907) 205-6902**, website **https://mrjcarrazco.github.io/afivestar/**
7. **Verification:** Google will offer postcard-by-mail, phone, email, or **video verification** (most common now for service businesses: they ask for a short video showing your work vehicle, tools, and a job in progress). Do whichever it offers. Postcard takes ~1-2 weeks to a mailing address (not shown publicly).
8. After verification, finish the profile (Edit profile):
   - **Hours:** e.g. Mon–Sat 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM (whatever is true).
   - **Description** (paste, edit freely): *"A Five Drywall is a family-run drywall and sheetrock contractor based in Wasilla, Alaska, serving the Mat-Su Valley and Anchorage. We handle hanging, taping and mudding, texture and texture matching, level 5 smooth finishes, and drywall repair — from single-room patches to full new-construction houses. Free estimates. Se habla español."*
   - **Attributes:** check "Identifies as Latino-owned" if you want (helps some searches), "Free estimates" if offered.
   - **Services list:** Drywall installation, Drywall repair, Taping and mudding, Texture application, Texture matching, Level 5 finish, Popcorn ceiling removal, Water damage drywall repair.
   - **Opening date:** the real year the family business started.

## Photos to take with your phone (upload 10+; profiles with photos get far more calls)
- Your work van/truck (even without lettering).
- Tools laid out / mud, taping tools, stilts — "real crew" proof.
- Before/during/after of a repair: hole → patched → textured → painted (4 shots, same wall).
- A finished room: clean seams, corner detail close-up.
- A texture close-up (orange peel / knockdown).
- You or family members working (faces optional).
- **Do NOT** upload stock photos or other people's work. Google detects it and customers can tell.
- Add 2–3 new photos every month — recency matters.

## Posts cadence (5 min each, from the GBP app/dashboard)
- **1 post per week** is ideal; every 2 weeks minimum. A post = 1 photo + 2–3 sentences + "Call now" button.
- Rotation that works: (wk1) finished-job photo, (wk2) tip ("Cracks come back every spring? Here's why…"), (wk3) service spotlight ("We match knockdown texture so patches disappear"), (wk4) ES-language post ("Presupuestos gratis — se habla español").
- Never claim jobs you didn't do; never mention customers by name without asking.

## Reviews (the real ranking fuel)
- After EVERY happy job: open GBP app → "Ask for reviews" → it gives you a short link → text it to the customer while you're still friendly and top-of-mind.
- Say: "If you're happy with the work, a quick Google review really helps our family business."
- Reply to every review (a one-line thank-you counts). Never pay for or fake reviews.
- 5 real reviews likely puts you ahead of every drywall profile in Wasilla.
