## English

**1. Standard ask (day job is finished)**
> Hi {{NAME}}, this is Juan with A Five Star Drywall. Thanks again for having us out — it was a pleasure working on your {{JOB, e.g. "ceiling repair"}}. If you were happy with how it turned out, a quick Google review would mean a lot to our family business: {{GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK}}
> Takes about a minute. Thanks either way, and call us anytime at (907) 205-6902.

**2. Short version**
> Hi {{NAME}} — Juan from A Five Star Drywall. Glad we could get your {{JOB}} done! If you have 60 seconds, an honest Google review helps our small Wasilla business more than any ad: {{GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK}} Thank you!

**3. Follow-up (one week later, only if no review and they were clearly happy)**
> Hi {{NAME}}, Juan from A Five Star Drywall — no pressure at all, just wanted to leave this link once more in case it got buried: {{GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK}} Thanks again for the work, and we're here if anything ever needs a touch-up.

**4. Photo permission add-on (append to any of the above)**
> Also — would you mind if we used a before/after photo of the work on our website? No address or name shown, just the walls.

## Rules
- Personalize {{NAME}} and {{JOB}} every time — templates that read like templates get ignored.
- Ask everyone; never filter to only happy-looking customers *by offering different links* (review-gating violates Google policy). A simple "if you were happy" framing is fine.
- Never pay, discount, or gift for reviews.
- Save every review screenshot — real quotes (with permission) will replace the labeled sample testimonials on the site.
