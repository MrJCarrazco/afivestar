### Honest read
- **One serious digital competitor (Image Drywall & Paint).** They're 20+ years established, commercial-heavy, and have real SEO. We will not outrank them for "drywall Wasilla" overnight — and don't need to.
- **Everyone else is thin or invisible.** Most licensed drywall outfits in the valley have no website and (as far as searches show) no active Google Business Profile. Long-tail queries ("drywall repair Wasilla", "texture matching Palmer", "sheetrock contratista español") are wide open.
- **The gap Image leaves open:** small residential repair jobs, fast response, bilingual EN/ES service, and Google Business Profile map-pack presence for *repair* (they skew new-construction/commercial). The map pack for "drywall repair near me" in Wasilla is winnable with a verified GBP + a handful of real reviews, because so few competitors have profiles at all.
- **Proof the channel converts:** A & A Wall Solutions built a business on 30 HomeAdvisor reviews with no website. Reviews + directory presence = real leads in this market.

### Strategy in one line
Don't fight Image head-on for "drywall Wasilla" — win the map pack and the long tail (repair, texture matching, bilingual, Palmer/Big Lake/Houston) where nobody is competing, and convert with instant click-to-call and a friction-free quote form.
